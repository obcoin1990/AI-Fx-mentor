## KEY FINDINGS AT A GLANCE

### The Core Problem We Solve
Traders spend 15-20 minutes manually analyzing each chart (slow, emotional, inconsistent). AI Chart Mentor does this in <10 seconds with an explanation (fast, objective, consistent).

### Table Stakes (MVP Must Have)
1. Trend detection
2. Support/Resistance zones
3. Trade entry/SL/TP suggestions
4. Risk-reward calculation
5. **Mentor explanation (PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATOR)**
6. <10 second response time

### Why We Win vs. Competitors
- vs. TradingView: Instant AI analysis (not manual)
- vs. Signal services: Transparent explanation (not black box)
- vs. Other AI tools: Clear mentor teaching (not vague predictions)
- vs. Broker tools: Cross-platform (not locked to one broker)
- vs. Manual analysts: 24/7 instant (not timezone-dependent)

### Accuracy Targets (Non-Negotiable)
- Trend direction: 90%+
- S/R zone accuracy: 85%+ (must be ±2% of visible swing points)
- Trade idea viability: 80%+ (traders would actually take the setup)
- Explanation clarity: 80%+ (traders understand the reasoning)

### Success Metrics (Track Daily Post-Launch)
- Week 1: 50+ analyses/day, <10s latency, <2% errors, 70%+ positive feedback
- Week 2-4: 100+ analyses/day, 30%+ 7-day retention, 85%+ accuracy audit pass

### What NOT to Build (Phase 1)
- User accounts / history
- "90% accuracy" claims (legal liability)
- Multi-timeframe analysis (scope creep)
- Crypto/Stocks (Forex only)
- Automated trading / signals (regulatory risk)
- Alerts/notifications (only useful if 99% accurate)
